# Bounded prior-art comparison

REFERENCES PARTIAL. NOVELTY INCONCLUSIVE. Not human peer-reviewed.
The following source findings are inherited author-side comparisons, with
the later primary-text addendum superseding the v0.4 abstract-only limit.

- Zhang (2024), author revision dated 30 July 2025, Remark 7: finite
  observations already describe the period-two phenomenon. No discovery
  claim for that phenomenon is made here. The potential contribution is
  the all-odd-c proof for a=3, the least preperiod, and explicit nim-words.
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2023.114350
- Flammenkamp dissertation (submitted November 1996, catalogued 1997):
  relevant passages of the author-hosted original were compared. Four-move
  explicit nim-word construction is already known as a method. No direct
  anticipation was identified in the examined passages. The enlarged book
  edition, ISBN 3-932136-10-1, has NOT been examined in full.
  https://wwwhomes.uni-bielefeld.de/achim/diss.ps.gz
  https://wwwhomes.uni-bielefeld.de/achim/publications.html
- Moriwaki (2026): Theorem 2 requires the full-set adjacent-gap condition,
  which fails at 7>3+3. Theorem 4 instead uses an admissible base B and adds
  b1+br, 2b1+br, ... . For l=0 the target violates the gap bound; for l>=1
  the first adjoined move is even, whereas every target move is odd. This
  excludes only direct instantiation of that construction, not all indirect
  consequences. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11658
- Inoue-Inazu (2026): subsequent HTML full-text search and targeted reading,
  including Theorem 2.9 and the Grundy discussion, identified no direct
  theorem supplying this integer-heap sharp-preperiod formula. This is not
  proof that no indirect consequence exists.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09157
